# Journal

The journal is a personal lab diary and archive for LinkedIn posts related to the homelab. Each entry documents a significant change, experiment, or reflection.

---

## Purpose

- **Lab diary:** Record what was built, configured, or learned — including problems encountered and how they were solved.
- **LinkedIn archive:** Store published posts about the homelab for future reference.

---

## Entry Format Template

```markdown
---
date: YYYY-MM-DD
tags: [tag1, tag2]
linkedin: true
---

# Title

Content here.
```

- `date`: ISO 8601 date
- `tags`: relevant topics (e.g., `networking`, `kubernetes`, `iot`, `monitoring`)
- `linkedin: true` if the entry was or will be published as a LinkedIn post
