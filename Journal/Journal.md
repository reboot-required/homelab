## Writing an entry

Use the `Journal Entry` template. Title is `YYYY-MM-DD Title`; set
`linkedin: true` if it was or will be published.

Entries are dated records. Do not rewrite an old one to match what you later
learned — add a new entry and link back. The wrong turns are the interesting
part.
